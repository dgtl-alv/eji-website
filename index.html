Electric motorcycle charging

Charge your electric motorcycle. Pay only for the kWh you use.

PT Electra Jaringan Indonesia (EJI) holds an Electricity Supply Business License (IUPTL) and operates the ALVA charging network. Plug in, charge, and settle one daily bill by QRIS or GoPay.

How it works Contact us

Our service

We sell electricity for charging battery electric two-wheelers at our stations.

Charging stations

1,037 charging connectors across the ALVA network (November 2026), ready for ALVA electric motorcycles.

Pay per kWh

You pay for the energy delivered to your vehicle, as recorded by the station meter. Rates are itemised in your daily bill.

One daily digital bill

All charging sessions in a day are combined into one bill, sent by email and paid online.

How it works

Register once, then just plug in and charge.

1

Register once

Register your vehicle and accept the Terms and Conditions in the My ALVA app.

2

Plug in and charge

Plug in at any EJI station. The energy delivered is recorded automatically for each session.

3

Get your bill

Your daily bill is issued at 00:10 WIB and sent to your registered email, with kWh and charges per session.

4

Pay

Pay by QRIS or GoPay from the link in your bill. A QRIS code is valid for 15 minutes and can be regenerated.

Payment methods

QRIS GoPay
Payments are processed by Midtrans as our payment gateway. EJI does not store your card details, PIN, or e-wallet password.

Questions about bills, payments, or a station issue.
