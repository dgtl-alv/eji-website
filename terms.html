1. Parties

These Terms and Conditions govern the use of charging stations operated by PT Electra Jaringan Indonesia ("EJI") by registered users ("User"). By registering and using a station, the User confirms that they have read and accepted these terms.

2. Registration

Users register their vehicle and an active email address in the My ALVA app. Acceptance of these terms is given once and applies to all later sessions. Users are responsible for the accuracy of the data they register.

3. Use of stations

  • Stations may only be used to charge compatible vehicles, following the instructions on site.
  • The energy recorded by the station meter is the basis for billing.
  • Users must report station damage or problems to customer service.

4. Rates and billing

Charges are calculated per kWh of energy delivered, plus applicable taxes. Current rates are shown in the My ALVA app. All sessions in one calendar day are combined into one bill, issued at 00:10 WIB the next day and sent to the User's email. EJI may change its rates with at least [7] days' notice.

5. Payment

Bills are paid by QRIS or GoPay, processed by Midtrans. One payment settles one bill. A QRIS code is valid for 15 minutes; if it expires, the User can generate a new one from the bill link. Bills left unpaid for [X days] may lead to charging access being suspended until the bill is settled.

6. Cancellation and refund

Charging that has already taken place cannot be cancelled. A refund is given if:

  • the User has paid the same bill more than once; or
  • there is an error in the recorded kWh or bill calculation, confirmed by EJI.

Complaints must be sent to customer service within [7] days of the bill date, with the bill number and proof of payment. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method or the User's bank account within [14] business days.

7. Limitation of liability

EJI works to keep stations operational but does not guarantee that every station is available at all times. EJI is not liable for losses caused by use of a station contrary to its instructions, or by events beyond EJI's reasonable control.
