Privacy Policy

Effective from 25 November 2026

Data we collect

  • Identity and contact data: name, email, phone number.
  • Vehicle data: the vehicle identification number (VIN) of registered vehicles.
  • Usage data: station location, session time, and kWh delivered.
  • Transaction data: bill number, amount, payment method and status. Card details, PINs, and e-wallet passwords are processed by Midtrans and are not stored by EJI.

How we use it

We use this data to calculate and send bills, process payments, handle complaints and refunds, keep the service secure, and meet our reporting obligations to regulators.

Sharing

EJI shares data only with parties needed to run the service: our payment gateway (Midtrans), ALVA group affiliates that support operations, and government authorities where required by law. EJI does not sell personal data.

Retention and security

Data is kept while the account is active and for as long as tax and electricity regulations require, and is then deleted or anonymised. EJI applies reasonable technical and organisational safeguards in line with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting cs@electrajaringan.co.id. Requests are processed within the time required by applicable law.
